Backup coverage

The full backup includes Chart of Accounts, journal vouchers and all JV lines, currencies and exchange rates, budgets, and the app's backup metadata. Restore replaces the current local accounting dataset after confirmation.

Automatic backup limitation: Google access tokens expire. After the token expires or the app is reopened, reconnect to Google Drive to resume after-entry backups. This browser-only version cannot silently refresh Google access in the background. Keep a separate backup copy as an additional safeguard.

Use the app over HTTPS or localhost for Google sign-in. Opening the HTML directly as a file may not be accepted as an authorized Google OAuth origin.
